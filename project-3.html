Tiny Forest Explorers Website Design (Collaboration)

Project Type
UI/UX Design
Date
July 2026
Tools used
Figma, ChatGPT

Objective

Our team aimed to address the evolving digitisation of learning in schools by developing a web-based learning platform for elementary school students as a modern method of promoting engagement with their academics.

Methods

Inspired by earlier learning platforms like IXL for math and BrainPOP for science, we opted to focus on how schools could meet the learning expectations and digital habits of young children growing up in a technological society. We also wanted to explore the ways modern technology could supplement what students learn in school and further their interest in learning by presenting information in the form of stories, games, and compelling audio-visual media.

With our team of marketing specialists, a 3D animator, an AI artist, and a UI/UX designer, we determined what problem we wanted to address, our target users (i.e. children ages 8-12, educators, and parents), and created personas and proto-personas as a starting point.

Through user interviews, we met with willing participants that aligned with our personas’ characteristics to gain insights regarding the features and content they deemed appropriate for a learning platform like the one we were proposing, as well as honest reviews of our prototype.

Result

Despite participants only being shown a limited-functioning prototype, the feedback was overwhelmingly positive. Parents, children, and educators said they could see themselves using our platform at home and in their classrooms as a way to supplement the subjects students are learning in school.

Some feedback points included implementing collaborative features so users can connect with one another, a dashboard for educators to align the content with their lesson plans, and progress indicators so parents and educators and monitor students’ learning progress.

Future Considerations

While implementing the feedback we received was outside of the scope for this project, the team recognises the value of providing parents and educators with more control over the platform so each student’s learning experience is matches their progress in school.

In future iterations, we would include teacher-focused features, more content, and social activity options in an effort to bring traditional learning to the digital realm and help foster young children’s desire to learn. We would also consider organisations that work with schools as key stakeholders as well.
