FinTech App Design

Project Type
UI/UX Design
Date
April 2026
Tools used
Figma

Objective

My mobile app design aims to streamline digital finance for freelancers, allowing them to efficiently manage their invoices, income, expenses, bills, and more.

Methods

I began by researching my target users and the most popular finance platforms that offer features targeted to freelancers’ needs. Through design sprints, I put together an initial colour palette, dashboard designs, low-fidelity wireframes for the desktop and mobile landing pages, an information architecture diagram, and a key user flowchart. I later developed my wireframes into mid-fidelity prototypes in Figma.

Once my prototypes were functional and visually appealing, I conducted remote moderated user testing on peers using the time-on-task and read-aloud methods, as well as administered UMUX-Lite surveys to evaluate the my prototype’s usability.

Result

My observation notes from usability testing revealed some pain-points, including:

  • Most participants clicking the “Invoices” widget when it didn’t link to anything
  • Occasional unclear text labels and headings
  • Expectations that the “invoice” was “sent” before participants reached the confirmation page
  • A too-subtle confirmation message on the confirmation page

In my next iteration, based on feedback and observations, I linked the “Invoices” widget to the Invoices page. I also added a hover state that communicated how users could click the widget to reach the Invoices page directly.

I also reworded key menu items, page headings, buttons, and system state changes to clearly convey what the user was supposed to do or expect. This adjustment helped better align my prototype with real-world language and system states participants were already familiar with (Nielsen Norman’s 2nd heuristic).

Finally, I put together a thorough design system containing my colour palette, typography settings, and all of my Figma components, which I used as a guide while creating the experience to ensure consistency throughout the design.

Future Considerations

In future iterations of this experience, I would aim to develop the desktop version of this design to create a cohesive system that could be useful to freelancers whether they access the platform from their phone or from a laptop.
