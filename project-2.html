Duolingo App Redesign (Collaboration)

Project Type
UI/UX Design
Date
November 2025
Tools used
Figma

Objective

My partner and I aimed to address usability issues we discovered within the Duolingo app after conducting an evaluation using Nielsen Norman’s 10 Usability Heuristics.

Methods

Basing our proposed changes according to Nielsen Norman’s 10 Usability Heuristics, we found some layout-related inconsistencies depending on the selected language, confusion during orientation for new users, and occasional unclear language.

In our design, we proposed including a symbol so users knew where to click to select the language they want to learn, including a tutorial and explanations of the app’s different functions and features, and adding feedback when the system’s state changes (ex. Purchase item —> Purchased successfully).

After our initial prototype was complete, we conducted moderated, remote testing sessions with peers to identify any pain-points they discovered while using our prototype and how new users would be able to navigate the app.

We used the read-aloud method, task-based testing, and a System Usability Scale questionnaire for participants to complete afterwards. During the test, we also recorded how quickly our participants completed the tasks we asked of them. These methods allowed our team to track where participants got lost, when they experienced hesitation, and users’ thought processes behind their decisions to click certain icons to reach certain pages or assuming specific buttons had certain functions.

Result

The majority of participants found the interface clean and navigable, easy to understand, and quick to learn. For instance, they easily associated the shopping bag icons with the shop and the question mark as an assistive features, as they matched their mental models.

However, certain icons we changed, like the initial language-selection icon, confused some users and ended up overcomplicating the experience.

With all the feedback collected, we made adjustments including simplifying the language-selection icon, adding a second language path that users can switch to, and adding buttons under the items in the shop to clarify when users could interact with their power-ups.

Future Considerations

Further refinement of this project would include an error prevention feature in the shop in the event a user accidentally clicks the purchase button. Additionally, we would increase the visual contrast throughout the design in order to clarify navigation even further. Some changes include increasing the weight of the text on the homepage and in the shop and containing smaller homepage icons within circles to improve visibility.
