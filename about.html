About me

Lucie is a digital media artist and designer with a passion for UI/UX and graphic design. With a fine arts background in New Media, she enjoys blending elements of visual art, design, interactive storytelling, and creative technologies to create memorable digital experiences that connect audiences. She strives to continue exploring the intersection of technology, art, and engaging narratives within her practice.

On her own time, she can be found painting with acrylics, reading cozy novels, doing crossword puzzles, and playing Stardew Valley.

Lucie holds a BFA in New Media and a Master's degree in Digital Media, both from Toronto Metropolitan University.​
